# M11-04 Known Risks

1. Story Knowledge is a projection over existing authoritative records. Missing or weak upstream canon/state/relationship data can produce sparse visualization output; the projection must not fabricate facts to compensate.
2. Relationship graph, timelines and history views are authoring aids. Their visual arrangement is not persisted as story truth and must remain reconstructable.
3. Performance budgets are proven for the dedicated 100/300/1000 chapter fixtures. Real projects can contain denser entity and relationship distributions, so bounded limits and stable pagination must remain enforced.
4. Electron E2E and Windows native IME are materially slower than unit/integration suites and can experience hosted-runner variance. Merge authority remains the permanent CI gate rather than local timing observations.
5. Actions artifacts have retention windows. The committed Schema 2 manifest preserves integrity metadata, while workflow artifacts remain auxiliary diagnostics rather than the sole durable proof.
6. M11-05 and M11-06 may reuse the request-ownership, navigation and Story Knowledge contracts, but must not turn M11-04 projections into a second persistence authority.
7. `IMPLEMENTED` is a static closure state. Effective `VERIFIED` remains bound to PR #364's eventual merge commit and its task verification context.
